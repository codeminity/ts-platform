Adds a full layout system: `<cdmt-layout>` (view-string-driven header/footer/drawer positioning, auto-routes children by tag name), `<cdmt-header>`/`<cdmt-footer>` (fixed positioning, scroll-reveal), `<cdmt-drawer>` (overlay/docked/mini modes, responsive breakpoint-driven mobile behavior, `show()`/`hide()`/`toggle()`), `<cdmt-page-container>` and `<cdmt-page>` (content offset and min-height derived from the measured layout). All six ship with a Vue binding at `@codeminity/ui-kit/vue`.
